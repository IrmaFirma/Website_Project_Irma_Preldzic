THE STORY

Some doors change everything.

The core narrative of Trapped draws directly from a dream experienced by our teammate, Dina. In her dream, she found herself frantically running through a corridor, attempting to enter a locked room as time slipped away. When she finally breached the door, she was confronted by a figure speaking in an incomprehensible dialect. Our primary objective was to capture that visceral sense of disorientation, urgency, and psychological frustration, leaning into the specific, fragmented details that remained vivid in her memory after waking.

Working within strict time constraints proved to be our greatest challenge during production. Creative ideas continually emerged while shooting, prompting mid-scene adjustments that a more extended pre-production phase might have streamlined. These spontaneous choices added complexity to the editing stage, where fitting our footage into a tight 30-minute post-production window demanded rapid decision-making.

Ultimately, this project underscored the critical role of time management in visual storytelling, proving that production constraints can spark unexpected creative solutions. Navigating a sea of raw, unedited footage deepened our appreciation for the editorial process: specifically the skill required to transform chaotic, unrefined takes into a coherent, impactful story.
